# DOH FAQ-English

## Q1: Are healthcare services and medicines provided free of charge in government hospitals?

Yes, medical consultations, treatments, and routine medicines are provided free of charge to all citizens at government hospitals, clinics, and dispensaries across Sri Lanka.

## Q2: How can I report a public health issue, such as a Dengue mosquito breeding site?

You can report public health issues to the Public Health Inspector (PHI) or the Medical Officer of Health (MOH) office in your local area. Contact details can be obtained from your local council or the Ministry of Health website.

## Q3: Where can I get my child vaccinated according to the National Immunization Schedule?

Routine childhood vaccinations are administered free of charge at your area's Medical Officer of Health (MOH) clinics and maternal and child health clinics.
