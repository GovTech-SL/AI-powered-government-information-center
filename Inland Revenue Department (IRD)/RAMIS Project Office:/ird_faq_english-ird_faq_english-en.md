# IRD FAQ-English

## Q1: How can I register for a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN)?

You can register for a TIN online through the IRD e-Services portal (RAMIS system), by post, or by visiting the IRD Head Office or any regional office with your National Identity Card (NIC).

## Q2: How can I pay my taxes online?

You can pay taxes online via the Online Tax Payment Platform (OTPP) using the internet banking facilities provided by participating licensed commercial banks in Sri Lanka.

## Q3: What is the final date to submit the annual Income Tax Return?

The statutory deadline to submit the annual Return of Income is the 30th of November immediately succeeding the end of that specific year of assessment.
