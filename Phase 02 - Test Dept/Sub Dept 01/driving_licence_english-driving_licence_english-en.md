# Driving Licence-English

## Q1: What are the instructions for taking  new driving licence?

The DMT provides instructions for obtaining a new smart-card driving licence. 
There are two methods: 
1. Online method – available at the Head Office/Werahera and selected district offices. 
2. Offline method – available at other district offices. 
The official DMT page currently lists Kurunegala, Hambantota, Gampaha, Anuradhapura, 
Kalutara, Jaffna and Monaragala among the locations with online services.
