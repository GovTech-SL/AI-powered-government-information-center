How to know which trains are available at a time, you want to get your destination?

E.g. if you want to go to Hatton ,first  select Colombo-Badulla Line  then select the starting place (Maradana ),the destination Hatton), and  give  the time period. Lines Colombo to Badulla Colombo to Matale Colombo to Avisawella Colombo to Matara Colombo to Baticaloa Colombo to Trincomale Colombo to Puttalam Matale to Badulla Colombo to Mihintale

How could we forward our suggestions and inquiries to The Railway department

If you have  valuable  suggestions as well as inquiries  ,just  email us. your inquiries will be attented immediately and reply you as early as possible and worthful suggetions will be forwarded to top management for cosideration in taking decisions for the betterment of the train service.
