How to apply for rent benefit in Sri Lanka

The Rent Benefit Scheme is intended to provide temporary financial assistance to eligible tenants who are
facing hardship in meeting their monthly rental payments. Below is a sample procedure outlining the steps
an applicant would generally follow.
1. Visit the nearest Divisional Secretariat office and request the Rent Benefit application form.
2. Fill in the application form with your personal details, current address, and landlord's
information.
3. Attach copies of your National Identity Card, the signed rental agreement, and proof of
monthly income.
4. Submit the completed application along with the supporting documents to the designated
officer.
5. Attend a verification interview if scheduled, and provide any additional documents requested.
6. Wait for the eligibility assessment to be completed by the review committee.
7. Once approved, the benefit amount will be credited to your registered bank account on a
monthly basis.
8. Renew your application annually or as required by the scheme's guidelines.
