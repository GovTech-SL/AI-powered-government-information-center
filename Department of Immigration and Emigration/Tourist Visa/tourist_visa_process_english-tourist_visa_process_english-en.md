# Tourist Visa Process-English

## Q1: Q1: How do I apply for a Tourist Visa?

A: You can apply online through the official e-Visa portal by submitting your passport details, travel itinerary, and paying the required processing fee online before your departure.

## Q2: Q2: What documents are required to apply for a Tourist Visa?

A: You need a passport valid for at least 6 months from your intended date of entry, a confirmed return travel ticket, proof of sufficient funds for your stay, and a recent passport-sized photograph.

## Q3: Q3: How long is a standard Tourist Visa valid?

A: Standard tourist visas are typically issued for an initial stay of up to 30 or 60 days upon entry, depending on your nationality and visa scheme.

## Q4: Q4: How long does it take to process the visa application?

A: Online e-Visa applications are generally processed within 24 to 72 hours. However, it is recommended to submit your application at least one week prior to your travel date.

## Q5: Q5: Can I extend my Tourist Visa while in the country?

A: Yes, tourist visas can be extended beyond the initial entry period up to a maximum permitted limit. Applications for extensions must be submitted to the Department of Immigration and Emigration or through their official online portal before your current visa expires.
