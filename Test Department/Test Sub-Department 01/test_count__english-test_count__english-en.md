If you are applying for or renewing a passport through the Department of Immigration and Emigration, you need the following original documents and photocopies: [2, 3] 

**National Identity Card (NIC):** Original NIC with a clear photocopy. [2, 3] 

**Birth Certificate:** Original Birth Certificate of the applicant with a photocopy. [2, 3] 

**Marriage Certificate:** Original marriage certificate (if applicable, to confirm a name change after marriage). [2, 3] 

**Current/Previous Passport:** Current passport with a photocopy of the bio-data page (if you already possess one). [2, 3] 

**Profession Documents:** Educational or academic certificates related to your profession, plus a service/employment confirmation letter. [3] 

**Photo Studio Acknowledgement:** Digital photograph acknowledgement from an authorized photo studio (photos must be taken within the last 6 months). [3, 4] 

**Contact Info:** A valid mobile number and email address for online profile registration. [2] 

**Biometrics:** Personal attendance at the Head Office (Battaramulla), a regional office (Matara, Kandy, Vavuniya, Kurunegala), or a designated Divisional Secretariat (DS-DRP) unit is mandatory for fingerprint and digital capture. [2, 5]
