## Article 1: Obtaining a Certified Copy of a Birth Certificate

Department: Registrar General’s Department.

Service name: Certified copy of a birth certificate. People also call it a birth extract.

Who can apply: The person named on the certificate, a parent of that person, a legal guardian, or an authorised representative who carries a signed authority letter from the person named on the certificate.

Fee: Rs. 500 for each certified copy. This fee has been in effect since 1 March 2025.

Processing time: 14 working days for the normal service. The urgent service issues the copy on the same day for an additional fee of Rs. 1,000, which makes the total Rs. 1,500.

Documents required: The applicant’s National Identity Card (NIC) or passport, and the birth details of the person named on the certificate. The birth details are the full name, date of birth, place of birth and the names of both parents.

Where to apply: At the Divisional Secretariat, at the District Registrar’s office, or online at https://www.rgd.gov.lk.

How to apply step by step: First, fill the application form. Second, attach a copy of your NIC or passport. Third, pay the fee at the counter or online. Fourth, collect the certificate from the issuing office, or receive it by registered post if you chose postal delivery.

### Urgent issuance of birth certificate copies

The urgent service is available only at the District Registrar’s office. Applications for the urgent service must be submitted before 12.00 noon to receive the copy on the same working day. The urgent service is not available online.

## Article 2: Obtaining a Certified Copy of a Marriage Certificate

Department: Registrar General’s Department.

Service name: Certified copy of a marriage certificate.

Who can apply: Either spouse, or an authorised representative with a signed authority letter from one of the spouses.

Fee: Rs. 500 for each certified copy. This fee has been in effect since 1 March 2025.

Processing time: 14 working days for the normal service.

Documents required: The applicant’s NIC or passport, the marriage registration number, and the district where the marriage was registered.

Where to apply: At the Divisional Secretariat or at the District Registrar’s office of the district where the marriage was registered. The online service is available at https://www.rgd.gov.lk.

## Article 3: National Identity Card Services

Department: Department for Registration of Persons.

### Applying for a new National Identity Card

Who can apply: Sri Lankan citizens who are 15 years of age or older. Applicants under 18 must apply through their school or with a parent or guardian.

Fee: The first National Identity Card is issued free of charge.

Processing time: 30 working days for the normal service. The one-day service costs Rs. 2,500 and is available only at the head office in Battaramulla.

Documents required: The original birth certificate, a Grama Niladhari certificate confirming residence, and two recent passport-size photographs. If you do not have a birth certificate, see Article 1 on how to obtain a certified copy.

Where to apply: At the Divisional Secretariat of your area. Online applications are accepted at https://www.drp.gov.lk.

### Replacing a lost or damaged National Identity Card

Fee: Rs. 2,000 to replace a lost card, and Rs. 1,000 to replace a damaged card. These fees have been in effect since 1 June 2025.

A police complaint report is required for a lost card. It is not required for a damaged card, but the damaged card must be handed in at the time of application.

Processing time for a replacement card is 30 working days for the normal service.

## Article 4: Ordinary Passport Services

Department: Department of Immigration and Emigration.

Service name: Ordinary passport. Many people also call it a travel document or a travel booklet.

### Eligibility

Any Sri Lankan citizen may apply for an ordinary passport. Applicants under 16 years of age must apply together with a parent or legal guardian. Both parents’ consent is needed for a minor, and one parent may give written consent if the other parent is not present.

### Fees and validity

Fee for the normal service: Rs. 10,000 for a 32-page passport. Fee for the one-day service: Rs. 20,000 for a 32-page passport. Fees have been in effect since 1 July 2025.

Validity: An ordinary passport is valid for 10 years from the date of issue.

Processing time: 20 working days for the normal service. The one-day service issues the passport on the same day when the application is submitted at the head office in Battaramulla before 10.00 in the morning.

### Documents required

The applicant must bring the National Identity Card, the original birth certificate or a certified copy of it, two recent passport-size photographs, and the filled application form. If the applicant does not have a certified copy of the birth certificate, it can be obtained from the Registrar General’s Department as described in Article 1.

### How to apply

Book an appointment online at https://www.immigration.gov.lk. Attend the appointment with your documents. Pay the fee. Give your fingerprints and photograph at the counter. Collect the passport on the date given on your receipt.

### Renewing a passport

A passport can be renewed up to 6 months before it expires. The renewal fee is the same as the fee for a new passport. The old passport must be handed in at the time of renewal.

### Lost or stolen passport

If a passport is lost or stolen, report the loss at the nearest police station as soon as possible and obtain the police report. Then apply for a replacement at the Department of Immigration and Emigration with the police report, your National Identity Card and the application form. A replacement passport carries an additional penalty fee of Rs. 15,000 on top of the normal fee. The replacement takes 30 working days because the department must verify the loss and cancel the old passport record.

## Article 5: Driving Licence Renewal

Department: Department of Motor Traffic.

Who can apply: Any holder of a valid or recently expired Sri Lankan driving licence.

When to renew: A driving licence can be renewed up to 6 months before it expires.

Fee: Rs. 1,000 for a renewal. A late fee of Rs. 500 applies if the licence has already expired.

Validity: A renewed driving licence is valid for 8 years.

Medical requirement: Holders who are 60 years of age or older must submit a medical certificate that is not older than 3 months.

Processing time: 10 working days. A licence holder can also use the same-day renewal service at the head office in Werahera.

Where to apply: At the Department of Motor Traffic or at a regional office. Information is available at https://www.dmt.gov.lk.

## Article 6: Police Clearance Certificate

Department: Sri Lanka Police.

Who can apply: Sri Lankan citizens and foreign residents who need a clearance certificate for employment, immigration or study.

Fee: Rs. 1,000 when the certificate is for use inside Sri Lanka. Rs. 3,500 when the certificate is for use abroad.

Processing time: 7 working days. This was changed by a circular issued in 2025.

Documents required: The National Identity Card or passport, and a letter from the employer or institution that requested the certificate.

Where to apply: At the police station of your area. Information is available at https://www.police.lk.

### Older circular on police clearance processing time (issued in 2023)

A circular issued in 2023 stated that the processing time for a police clearance certificate is 14 working days. The 2023 circular does not mention the later 2025 circular.

## Article 7: Duplicate Examination Certificates

Department: Department of Examinations.

Service name: Duplicate certificate. People also call it a replacement certificate or a certified copy of results.

Who can apply: The candidate named on the certificate.

Fee: Rs. 1,500 for a duplicate of a General Certificate of Education Ordinary Level certificate. Rs. 2,000 for a duplicate of an Advanced Level certificate.

Processing time: 21 working days.

Documents required: The National Identity Card, the candidate’s index number and the year of the examination. If the original certificate was lost, a police complaint report is also required.

Where to apply: Online at https://www.doenets.lk or at the Department of Examinations head office in Pelawatta.

## Article 8: Notice of Revised Certificate Fees

Department: Registrar General’s Department.

This notice announces that from 1 January 2027 the fee for a certified copy of a birth certificate and the fee for a certified copy of a marriage certificate will increase from Rs. 500 to Rs. 750 for each copy. The urgent service surcharge for birth certificate copies will remain Rs. 1,000. Fees remain Rs. 500 until 31 December 2026.

## Article 13: General Guidance on Preparing for Government Service Applications

Before visiting any government office, check the official website of the relevant department for the latest fees and opening hours. Carry original documents and one set of photocopies. Most Divisional Secretariats are open from 8.30 in the morning to 4.15 in the afternoon on working days and are closed on weekends and public holidays.

The National Identity Card is the most commonly accepted proof of identity for government services. When a National Identity Card is not available, a valid passport may be accepted for services that allow it.

Fees must be paid at the counter, through the department’s official online payment page, or at an authorised post office where the department allows it. Receipts must be kept until the service is completed.
