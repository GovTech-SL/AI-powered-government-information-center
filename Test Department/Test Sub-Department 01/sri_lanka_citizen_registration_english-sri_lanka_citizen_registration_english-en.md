**1. Civil Registration (Birth, Marriage, & Death)**

**Responsible Body:** Registrar General’s Department (RGD) & Divisional Secretariats.

**Services Available:** Registration of births, deaths, and marriages; issuing certified copies.

**Basic Application Steps:**

Obtain the relevant application form (Form B4 for Births, Form M for Marriages, Form D for Deaths) from your Divisional Secretariat or Grama Niladhari (GN).

Fill out details and attach verified support documents.

Submit to the Registrar of the division where the event occurred.

**Required Documents:** Identity card of applicants, hospital notification of birth/death, marriage notice/declaration forms.

**Official Portal & Contact:**  | Tel: 1919 (Government Information Center) / +94 11 288 9500.

**Fees & Processing Time:** Standard certified copy fee: LKR 100–200. Processing: 1–3 working days (over the counter).

**2. National Identity Cards (NICs)**

**Responsible Body:** Department for Registration of Persons (DRP).

**Services Available:** First-time issuance (age 15+), renewals, lost/damaged replacements, details updates.

**Basic Application Steps:**

Obtain DRP application form via Grama Niladhari.

Get ICAO-compliant digital photographs taken at an authorized studio (Studio Reference Number required).

Get the application attested by the Grama Niladhari and Divisional Secretary.

Submit to DRP Head Office (Battaramulla) or regional offices (Galle, Kurunegala, Vavuniya, Batticaloa).

**Required Documents:** Birth Certificate (original), GN endorsement, studio photo receipt, old NIC (if renewing), police report (if lost).

**Official Portal & Contact:**  | Tel: +94 11 522 6100 / 1919.

**Fees & Processing:** Standard service: ~LKR 200–500; One-Day Service: ~LKR 1,000–2,000 (subject to official update).

**3. Passports and Visas**

**Responsible Body:** Department of Immigration and Emigration (DIE).

**Services Available:** Issuance of all-country passports, renewals, alterations, emergency certificates, visas for foreigners.

**Basic Application Steps:**

Book an online appointment via the official DIE portal.

Complete the online or printed passport application form.

Submit photos taken at authorized studios (digital photo system).

Attend the appointment at Head Office (Battaramulla) or regional branches (Matara, Kandy, Vavuniya, Kurunegala).

**Required Documents:** Original Birth Certificate, Original NIC, photo acknowledgment note, marriage certificate (for married women changing surname).

**Official Portal & Contact:**  | Tel: +94 11 532 9000 / 1919.

**Fees & Processing:**

Normal Service: Approx. LKR 10,000 (Processing time: ~14–30 days).

Urgent Service: Approx. LKR 20,000 (Processing time: Same day / 24 hours).

**4. Legal Aid & Access to Courts**

**Responsible Body:** Legal Aid Commission (LAC) of Sri Lanka.

**Services Available:** Free legal advice, court representation for low-income citizens, mediation, public legal awareness.

**Basic Application Steps:**

Visit the nearest LAC center (located near major court premises island-wide).

Request an interview with an officer-in-charge to verify income eligibility.

Fill out the assistance request form.

**Required Documents:** Proof of income (Gramadhari/Divisional Secretariat certificate), NIC, case-related documents/summons.

**Official Portal & Contact:**  | Tel: +94 11 243 3618 / +94 11 244 7234.

**Assistance:** Free for qualifying low-income earners.

**7. Grassroots Administration: GN & Secretariats**

**Divisional Secretariat (DS):** Handles administrative services, permits, vehicle registrations, and social safety programs.

**Grama ****Niladhari**** (GN):** Local administrative officer assisting with residence certificates, character certificates, and local verification.

**8. Lost, Missing, or Errors in Official Documents**

**Lost Documents:** File a complaint at the nearest police station immediately to obtain a Police Report before applying for duplicates.

**Errors on Certificates:** Apply for correction (e.g., Section 27/52 amendments for birth certificates) at the Divisional Secretariat with supporting secondary proof (school leaving certificates, baptism records, old passports).
