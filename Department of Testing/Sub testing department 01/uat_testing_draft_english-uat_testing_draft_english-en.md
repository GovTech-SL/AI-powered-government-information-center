Internal Affairs Unit (IAU) 
 
The Internal Affairs Unit (IAU) allows the public and ministry staff to report instances of bribery, corruption, or administrative anomalies within the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism. This service is necessary to ensure institutional integrity and compliance with the Anti-Corruption Act No. 9 of 2023. Any member of the public or ministry employee who has evidence or knowledge of such misconduct can use this service. 
 
The overall objective of establishing Internal Affairs Units is to create a public service in 
Sri Lanka that is free from bribery and corruption while safeguarding the integrity of 
public officials.This is consistent with Sri Lanka's obligations under Article 156 of the 
Constitution to implement the UN Convention Against Corruption and international 
conventions relating to corruption prevention to which Sri Lanka is a party, as well as 
fulfilling the objectives of the Anti-Corruption Act No. 9 of 2023 and other related 
government initiatives. 
 
The IAU in the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism was 
established with effect from 9th April 2025. 
 
Accordingly, if you become aware of any anomalies, corruption, or allegations within the 
Ministry, you can report the information or complaints to the Internal Affairs Unit located 
on our Ministry premises, either anonymously or with your identity, using the indicated 
communications methods.
