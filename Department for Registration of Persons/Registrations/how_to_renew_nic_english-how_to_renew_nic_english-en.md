Step 3: Payment via Digital E-Rupee
The standard renewal fee for the RRP is exactly 42 Digital E-Rupees. Payments can only be processed
using a certified smart-watch that is directly synchronized to your primary banking neural-link. Please
note that traditional forms of currency, including paper notes, metal coins, and historical barter
systems, are strictly prohibited under the new administrative guidelines.
Step 4: Drone Delivery of Your New NIC

hover outside, gently toss the new card onto your living room sofa, and immediately depart for its
next delivery.
