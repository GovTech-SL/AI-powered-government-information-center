1 General 
What is a tax? 
A tax is a compulsory payment made to the 
Government to fund public services and government 
activities. 
2 TIN 
What is a TIN? 
A Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) is a unique 
identification number issued by the Inland Revenue 
Department (IRD) for tax-related purposes. 
3 TIN 
Who needs to obtain 
a TIN? 
Individuals and entities who are required to register 
with the IRD should obtain a TIN. The exact 
registration requirements depend on the applicable 
tax rules. 
4 Registration How can I register for 
a TIN? 
You can register with the Inland Revenue Department 
through the available IRD registration channels and 
provide the required identification and supporting 
information. 
5 Income Tax 
What is income tax? 
Income tax is a tax imposed on taxable income earned 
by individuals and entities, subject to the applicable 
Sri Lankan tax laws. 
6 Individual 
Do individuals have to 
pay income tax? 
An individual may be liable for income tax when their 
taxable income exceeds the applicable threshold or 
where other tax obligations apply. 
7 Employment Is my salary subject to 
tax? 
Employment income may be subject to applicable 
income-tax rules. The amount payable depends on 
the individual's taxable income and applicable reliefs, 
deductions and rates. 
8 Business 
Does a business need 
to register for tax? 
Businesses may need to register with the IRD 
depending on their business activities, turnover, legal 
structure and applicable tax requirements.

9 VAT 
What is VAT? 
Value Added Tax (VAT) is an indirect tax imposed on 
the supply of goods and services and imports, subject 
to the applicable VAT law. 
10 VAT 
Who needs to register 
for VAT? 
Businesses meeting the applicable VAT registration 
requirements must register with the IRD. 
11 VAT 
Do all businesses have 
to charge VAT? 
No. VAT obligations depend on factors such as the 
nature of the business, taxable supplies and 
applicable registration requirements. 
12 Filing 
What is a tax return? 
A tax return is a declaration submitted to the IRD 
containing information about a taxpayer's income, 
deductions, tax liability and other required 
information. 
13 Filing 
How do I submit my 
tax return? 
Taxpayers can submit returns through the channels 
provided by the IRD, including applicable electronic 
services. 
14 Payment 
How can I pay my 
taxes? 
Tax payments can be made through payment 
methods and channels approved by the IRD, subject 
to the type of tax and applicable payment 
instructions. 
15 Payment 
How can I check 
whether I have 
outstanding tax? 
A taxpayer can check their tax account and 
outstanding obligations through the relevant IRD 
services or by contacting the IRD. 
16 Refund 
What should I do if I 
have paid more tax 
than required? 
Where applicable, a taxpayer may request a refund or 
have the excess amount dealt with according to the 
applicable tax procedures. 
17 Late Payment 
What happens if I do 
not pay my tax on 
time? 
Late payment may result in interest, penalties or 
other consequences under the applicable tax 
legislation.
